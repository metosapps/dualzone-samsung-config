Help & support

A few useful answers.

For help, email chennoufo11@gmail.com. Include your phone model, Android version, DualZone version, and the steps that led to the problem. Don't include passwords, verification codes, or private files.

Can I create three or more copies of an app?

No. This edition uses one Android work profile for one additional instance of a supported app. Your original app remains in your personal profile.

Will every app work?

No. Compatibility depends on the device and the app. Some apps restrict managed profiles, require services that are unavailable there, or apply their own account limitations. DualZone cannot bypass those restrictions.

Why can't I create a work profile?

Android 11 or later is required. An existing managed profile, employer policy, device restrictions, or manufacturer limitations can prevent setup. Do not remove an employer-managed profile without authorization. Send the displayed error and your phone model to support.

What happens when I remove the work profile?

Android deletes its apps and their data. This is not the same as closing DualZone. Use each app's backup or export options first. Your personal-profile apps remain separate.

How do I keep vault files safe?

Set a device PIN, pattern, or password and use Android authentication to unlock the vault. Keep independent backups. Import does not remove the original file; export creates a decrypted copy. Export important files before clearing app data or uninstalling. Support cannot recover lost encryption keys.

Are purchases active?

No. The current Samsung edition does not offer an active purchase flow. Future products and prices, if introduced, will be shown before you confirm anything through the store.

Where can I download DualZone?

DualZone is being prepared for Galaxy Store. A verified store link will be added here when the listing is publicly available. We do not distribute self-updating APKs through this website.

How can I request deletion of information?

Local files and app data can be removed on your device. Email support about messages or other information you sent us. For information held by a cloned app, contact that app's provider. See our Privacy Policy for details.
