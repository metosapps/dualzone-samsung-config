Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · Samsung edition · Package: com.dualzone.app

MetosApps provides DualZone and this website. This policy explains the information processed by DualZone, the choices you have, and how to contact us at chennoufo11@gmail.com.

1. Information processed on your device

  • App and profile information: DualZone reads launchable app names, package identifiers, icons, and profile availability to show apps you can clone, create a work profile, install selected apps there, and launch them. Clone records and preferences are stored locally. DualZone does not transmit your installed-app list to a developer-operated server.
  • Vault files: files you explicitly select and their metadata are stored in encrypted app-private storage. The vault does not upload these files to a DualZone cloud service. Import leaves the original file in its original location. Export writes a decrypted copy to a destination you choose, which may be backed up or synced by that destination's provider.
  • Authentication: vault access uses Android's biometric or device-credential prompt. DualZone does not receive your fingerprint template, face template, or device PIN.
  • Preferences: settings, onboarding state, profile identifiers, and cached configuration remain in app storage. Android backup is disabled for DualZone's app data.

2. Work profile and cloned apps

DualZone acts as the profile owner of the work profile you authorize. This allows it to manage apps in that profile and remove the profile when you request it. It is not a service for reading your conversations or credentials. Cloned apps independently process their own account details, messages, contacts, purchases, and other information under their own permissions and privacy policies. Their network activity is not governed by this policy.

3. Configuration and website hosting

The app contacts GitHub-hosted endpoints to fetch configuration, such as feature availability, support links, maintenance notices, and store-update requirements. Configuration is cached locally and the control configuration is signature-verified. GitHub and its delivery providers receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and request metadata when these requests occur.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, contact forms, or cookies set by us. Hosting providers may still process access logs and technical request information. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

At the effective date of this policy, advertising, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics collection are disabled in the Samsung release configuration. The app contains advertising and telemetry integrations, but they are not currently enabled. We will update the relevant disclosures and provide any legally required notice or consent before enabling additional collection. A remote configuration switch does not replace required consent.

5. Payments

The current Samsung edition does not offer an active purchase flow. A billing permission may be declared for future Samsung IAP registration; that permission alone does not initiate a purchase or collect payment information. If purchases are introduced in a future reviewed release, their price and terms will be shown before confirmation. Samsung will process store payments under its own policies. We will update this policy before introducing purchase validation or entitlement processing that collects additional information.

6. Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your requests. Our support mailbox uses Gmail, so Google processes these messages as the email provider. See Google's Privacy Policy. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private vault files.

7. Purpose, sharing, and retention

We process information to provide the functions you request, maintain app reliability and security, answer support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in support and security subject to your rights, compliance with law, or consent where required. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in this release.

Local information remains until you delete it, clear app data, remove the relevant profile, or uninstall the app as applicable. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet legitimate recordkeeping or legal needs, then deleted or anonymized. GitHub and Google apply their own retention practices to data they process. Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable transfer arrangements.

8. Your choices and rights

You can choose which apps to clone and which files to import, revoke supported permissions in Android settings, export or delete vault files, and request removal of the work profile. Removing that profile permanently deletes its apps and data. Clearing DualZone data or uninstalling it can make vault files unrecoverable; export files first. Removing DualZone is not a substitute for explicitly checking that the work profile has been removed.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Email us to exercise these rights or raise a concern. We may need reasonable verification of your request. You may also contact your local data-protection authority. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete files held only on your device or information held independently by cloned apps.

9. Security and children

We use measures including local encryption for vault files, Android access controls, HTTPS requests, and signed operational configuration. No system is completely secure, and vault storage is not a backup service. DualZone is not directed to children under 13 or anyone below the applicable minimum age for independent use in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

10. Changes and contact

We will publish policy changes here and revise the effective date. Material changes will be accompanied by notice and consent where required; merely updating this page does not override your legal rights. For privacy and support requests, contact chennoufo11@gmail.com.
